MY RECOMMENDATION

#2 Supreme Mathematics as the backbone — with #1's gold-glow on the 9 suns

The premise is a 3D galaxy of 2,559 nodes, and three forces point the same way: crisp blueprint lines stay legible at that scale where heavy bloom smears; #2 reserves a whole hue channel for Phase-2.5 type-color (the rework conversation we just had); and the Five-Percenter frame is peak Wu without leaning on the logo.

But #2 alone risks reading cold. So I'd graft #1's cinema onto the hubs only: the 9 emcee-suns get gold crowns + a breathing bloom (the emotional wow), while the dust-field stays crisp blueprint points (the legibility + the headroom). #3's loud type and flat boldness aren't wasted either — they're the right system for the mobile trailer and the OG/share cards in Phases 3–4, where flat reads better small. So #3 can become the "2D companion skin," not the 3D loser.
